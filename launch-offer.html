Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no payment, purchase or subscription required. Your complimentary 1 kg pack is worth ₹120.

Who can reserve?

Households in serviceable PIN codes across South Delhi and North Delhi can reserve. We’ll check your PIN code before confirming your mobile with an OTP.

Why do you ask for an OTP?

The OTP confirms your mobile number and helps us keep the offer to one complimentary pack per household. You don’t need a password or payment details.

What if you don’t deliver to my area yet?

We’ll let you know if your PIN code isn’t serviceable yet. An unavailable area won’t create a reservation or use a complimentary pack.
